## theseus-b6vz: a held turn said aloud; a stopped turn never spoken as failed

**Found.** A held voice turn returns `Ok` with `stop_reason` `awaiting_confirm` (and `awaiting_confirm` set) or
`budget`; its reply was only its text, often nothing. A `/stop` on a voice turn whose step the stop refuses ends the
submit in an error (`StoppedAtStep`); the RPC error carries the turn error's class, not "stopped" (`TurnFailed.class`
is "stopped", but only on the event), so the spawned submit spoke `FAILED_TURN`.

Where the stop is known: (1) the place's own `/stop` (`Control::Stop`), synchronously, before it sends
`execution.stop`; (2) the `turn.failed` event of the call's own turn, class `stopped` (a stop from the CLI or the
cockpit). (1) is ordered before the submit's answer by construction; (2) is best effort, since the watch event and
the RPC answer travel separately.

**Changed** (`df14ec29`):
- `HELD_TURN` = "I need your answer in the text channel.", spoken after the reply's text (or alone when the text is
  empty) for a turn that `waits_on_operator`: once per held turn, since each voice turn's submit answers once. A
  continuation after the operator answers is not a voice turn's submit, so it is not repeated.
- `Call.stopped`, set by `Place::voice_stop` (called first in `Control::Stop`: runtime.rs, one line) and by
  `voice_heard` on the own turn's `turn.failed` class `stopped`; cleared at each voice submit.
- `spoken_end` chooses the text: empty when stopped (and when an `Ok` result's `stop_reason` is `stopped`, which the
  task did not ask for: a stopped turn's partial text was spoken before; say if that should stay), the reply plus
  `HELD_TURN` when held, `FAILED_TURN` on any other failure.

**Proved** (tests_reply.rs):
- `a_held_voice_turn_says_once_that_the_answer_is_in_text`: a private lounge, a scripted `fs_write` that asks; the
  engine gets exactly one `Reply` "Writing it now.\n\nI need your answer in the text channel.". Planted revert (the
  held arm off): fails at `tests_reply.rs:104`.
- `a_stopped_voice_turn_is_not_spoken_as_failed`: a voice turn whose model call fails, `/stop` before its answer: the
  reply is empty. `a_turn_failed_as_stopped_ends_its_voice_turn_in_silence`: the same through a `turn.failed` class

## theseus-d93y: a voice call that joins but never hears

**Found** (songbird 0.6.0, davey 0.1.4, read from the vendored sources):
- songbird keeps the `DaveSession` private, inside its driver tasks (`udp_rx`, `ws`): there is no public `is_ready`,
  status or epoch, and no event for the welcome. The journal's `dave_session { … is_ready: false, status: INACTIVE }`
  is the `udp_rx` runner's `#[instrument]` span, recorded when it starts, so it is the state at the connect, which is
  INACTIVE in a call that later works too: not diagnostic.
- `udp_rx` drops a DAVE packet silently, before any `RtpPacket` or `VoiceTick`, when the session is not ready, the
  SSRC has no user yet, or no decryptor exists for the user. So "packets arrived but none decrypted" is invisible on
  the RTP side.
- What songbird does show: `CoreEvent::RtcpPacket`, after transport decryption only (DAVE never encrypts RTCP). A
  sender report from an SSRC means it is sending media. That is the packet signal used here.
- A rejoin without leaving the call is not offered: no API asks for a fresh key package or a DAVE reset (`reinit`
  runs only on the voice gateway's own transitions). `Call::leave` then `Call::join` on the same `Call` keeps the
  driver and its global event handlers, and a fresh connection sends a fresh key package. `Driver::leave` fires
  `DriverDisconnect` with `DisconnectReason::Requested`, which the receive handler now ignores while a rejoin runs.

**Changed** (`5907aa76`):
- theseus-voice `songbird_io.rs`: `SsrcCount.reports` (RTCP sender reports per SSRC), counted from `RtcpPacket`;
  `Link` (`SongbirdIo::link`): `counts()`, and `rejoin(gap, within)`: leave, wait, counts zeroed (users kept), join,
  connect within `within`, the requested disconnect not passed to the engine as a drop. `examples/join.rs` prints the
  sender reports too.
- theseus-discord `runtime/voice/deaf.rs` (new): `Hearing` (ready, elapsed, present listed speakers from the gateway's
  voice states, per-SSRC counts with the speaking map's user) and the pure rule `deaf()`; `Line`, the seam (songbird's
  `Link`, or a test's fake); `watch`, spawned at `start_call`: every `deaf_after`, off the audio path, until a listed
  speaker decodes or the call ends. Deaf: `voice.deaf` row (why, elapsed_ms, attempt, place) on the place's session,
  `theseus.voice.deaf` by `theseus.voice.why`, health's `deaf_since_ms`, the first notice, one rejoin
  (2 s gap, 20 s connect), `voice.rejoined` (ok, error, elapsed_ms, place), `rejoins` = 1. Then: hears → "Rejoined: I
  can hear you now", `deaf_since_ms` cleared; deaf again or rejoin error → `voice.deaf` attempt 2 (deaf again only),
  `deaf_failed`, "Still can't hear: try /leave and /join", and the watch returns. No path rejoins twice.
- `runtime/voice.rs`: `mod deaf`, the spawn in `start_call`, the status resets at the join and the end; nothing else.
- theseus-protocol: `voice.deaf`, `voice.rejoined`; `VoiceStatus` fields and the line (`· deaf since 22:41:07 UTC,
  rejoined once`, `· deaf (rejoin failed)`, `· rejoined once` once it hears).
- theseus-core: `record_voice_deaf` and `VOICE_DEAF` (INSTRUMENTS 42 → 43); `VoiceConfig.deaf_after_secs` (default 10,
  0 refused) and its template line.

Why one commit for the four parts: the rule, the watch, the rows and notices, and the key each have no reader without
the others (the reader rule), and a rule with no caller fails clippy's dead code.

**The rule's "not ready" clause** is implemented and tested, but songbird gives it no input: `ready` is `Some(true)`
once anything decodes, else `None`. In a live deaf call the rule fires on "a listed speaker's sender reports arrive
and none of their audio decrypts". A listed speaker present and silent with DAVE broken is therefore not caught until
they speak (as the task's "a silent channel is not deaf" asks anyway). Reading readiness for real would need songbird
to expose its session (a fork or an upstream change), or a tracing layer that watches davey's "Welcomed to group"
line; I did neither.

## The live check (the maintainer's, on the owner's daemon after an install)

1. The usual case: from the private place, `/join` the voice channel with a person in it, and speak within ~10 s.
   Expect no `voice.deaf` row and no notice:
   `theseus ledger -k voice.deaf` (empty), `theseus ledger -k voice.rejoined` (empty), and `theseus health`'s voice
   line without "deaf" or "rejoined".
2. The deaf path: I found no reliable way to force a missing MLS welcome from outside. Two to try:
   - `/join` while the person's client is still connecting to the channel (press `/join`, then join the voice channel
     within a second), and speak at once;
   - with the bot in the call, have the person leave and rejoin repeatedly while speaking (each join is a DAVE
     transition).
   When it happens: within ~10 s of speaking, a `voice.deaf` row (`why` "undecrypted", `attempt` 1), the notice "I
   can't hear the call (…). Rejoining…", the bot leaving and rejoining the channel within ~5 s, a `voice.rejoined` row
   (`ok` true), then "Rejoined: I can hear you now" once the person speaks again, and health's line
   `· rejoined once ·`. A rejoin that is deaf too: a second `voice.deaf` (`attempt` 2), "Still can't hear: try /leave
   and /join", health `· deaf (rejoin failed) ·`, and no further leave or join.
3. To see the evidence itself, `cargo run -p theseus-voice --example join --features voice …` (as before) now prints
   "N sender reports" per SSRC beside frames and decoded: a deaf call shows sender reports climbing with 0 decoded.
   Worth checking once that the owner's client sends RTCP sender reports at all: if it doesn't, a deaf call shows
   0 everywhere and the rule (by design) calls it silent, not deaf.
