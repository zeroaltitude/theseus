## Step 2: theseus-qryz, the retraction rule (67ca9021)
**Found.** 4 false rights and 5 false stales as listed; `new_free` was correct but untested.
**Changed (score.py, strict untouched).** `the old`/`the former` moved out of the prefix list: they govern only with a retraction elsewhere in the clause. A quote after a citing verb (`wrote "moved from…`) cancels the prefix, so a quoted move is not a retraction. `no longer wrong/stale/…` is not a suffix retraction. A coordinated list (`JOIN`: or, and, then, and later, comma) shares its phrase: a prefix flows to later members, a suffix to earlier ones. `then/later/next from X` is a prefix; `earlier it was X` and `before the move it was X` bracket like `was X before`; `X are both retired`. The old value named twice in a clause is retracted where either naming is. The rest is listed under "The rule's reach" in bench/recall/README.md.
