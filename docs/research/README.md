# Research

What Theseus was measured against. These were written for the design, and checked in on 2026-10-01 so that the
README's claims can be traced to their evidence.

- **[harness-landscape.md](harness-landscape.md)**: "Theseus among the harnesses", how Theseus compares with the
  provider-made harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, and others) and the independent ones (OpenClaw, Cursor, Hermes, and
  others), as of 2026-10-01. It covers:
  - where the field converged on Theseus's shape;
  - the guarantees no other harness was found to make;
  - where Theseus is level, and where it trails;
  - the ideas worth borrowing, and what it all means for v1.
- **[landscape-providers.md](landscape-providers.md)**: the research report behind it on the provider-made
  harnesses, gathered from official docs, changelogs, release notes, and the GitHub API, with every source.
- **[landscape-independents.md](landscape-independents.md)**: the research report on the independent harnesses,
  from their docs and repositories, with every source.

Facts in these reports are as of their date. The field moves monthly, so check a claim against its source before
relying on it.
