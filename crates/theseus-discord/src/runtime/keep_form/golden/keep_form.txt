=== the card in the owner's direct messages
Type the value in your own terminal \(run \`theseus vault keep\`; nothing is shown as you type\), or press "Type the value here" to type it in a form in this chat. In the cockpit, the card has a field for the value.
A value typed in the form goes through Discord's servers on its way to Theseus, and the form shows what you type; the terminal does neither.
Keep a secret in the vault?
In the vault "Harbor": item "Lantern new", field "credential" \(a new item\).
You give the value yourself, where only you see it; it never goes to the model, to this card or to a record. Do not paste it into chat: a value typed in chat is already with the model's provider and in Theseus's record, and Theseus cannot take it back \(rotate it at its provider, then give the new one where it is typed\).
Theseus's reason, in its own words: "the lantern provider's key"
